# Welcome to Torcheye Games

We're thrilled to launch the official Torcheye Games website and share our journey with you. This marks the beginning of something special - a studio dedicated to crafting immersive, atmospheric experiences that challenge conventions and tell meaningful stories.

## Who We Are

Torcheye Games is an indie game studio founded with a singular vision: to create games that explore the interplay between light and darkness, both visually and thematically. Our name reflects this core philosophy - the torch that illuminates, the eye that perceives.

We're a small but passionate team (plus Timmie, our studio cat and unofficial creative director) working from our studio to bring unique gaming experiences to life.

## Our Philosophy

We believe that games are more than entertainment - they're a medium for exploration, expression, and emotional connection. Our development philosophy centers on:

- **Atmosphere First**: Every element should contribute to the mood and feeling
- **Meaningful Mechanics**: Gameplay that serves the narrative and theme
- **Visual Storytelling**: Let art and environment tell the story
- **Player Agency**: Respect the player's time and intelligence

## Introducing Chiaroscuro

Our debut title, **Chiaroscuro**, embodies everything we stand for as a studio. It's a puzzle-platformer where light and shadow aren't just visual elements - they're the very fabric of reality.

Players manipulate contrast to:
- Reveal hidden paths through shadow
- Create platforms from light
- Solve environmental puzzles
- Uncover a mysterious narrative

The game draws inspiration from Renaissance art techniques, particularly the chiaroscuro method of using strong contrasts between light and dark to achieve a sense of volume and dimensionality.
